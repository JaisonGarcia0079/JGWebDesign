PRODUCTION INFORMATION

Where THE LAST RED SHOULDER served as a tie in story to bridge two story arcs, this OVA served as a prequel to the entire story while introducing the viewer to characters introduced in THE LAST RED SHOULDER. Because it was released after the TV series finished broadcasting, many of the story elements in this OVA were already well known and accepted by longtime viewers, so much of the standard exposistion of a premiere episode was discarded in favor of complex foreshaowing that answers many of the questions provoked by THE LAST RED SHOULDER and subtle plot innuendos in the TV series at large.

This OVA was also was adapted into a light novel called THE FIRST RED SHOULDER by the OVA's scriptwriter, Souji Yoshikawa.

Release Date: March 19, 1988

Director: Ryosuke Takahashi

Studio: Sunrise

STORY

"A sudden order to transfer. My days as a nameless soldier in the Melkian Armored Trooper corps have ended, and I set out on my journey. A journey into hell."

After Chirico Cuvie is given orders to transfer to Planet Odon, he and all of the other new recruits are sent into a simulated battle to test their abilities. However, this "simulated battle" turns out to be a serious fight to eliminate those without the proper skills to survive. It was cannibalism. Chirico survives along with three others, despite the fact that he fought while injured. It soon becomes clear that this isn't the first time he's survived something of this caliber and incredible; a fact that Colonel Pailsen wishes to exploit.

What does this mean for Chirico? What is he?
