DESIGN GOALS



&#x09;The goal of my form was to have a sort of interactivity. I want people to share the types of robots

&#x09;or mechs they happen to like if they've seen any. The genre means a lot and I want more people to

&#x09;learn to love it too.



&#x09;The gallery is an extension of why I love each mech. There's plenty of screenshots and photos there to

&#x09;show what each robots looked like in their respective shows or movies and how they captivated me.



&#x09;I mainly had a uniform look to the gallery. Everything looked uniform and same-ey across the board

&#x09;so it doesn't become too complicated.
