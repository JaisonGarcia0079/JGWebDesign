About Mecha

Mechanical Designs for anime have been around since the 1960's and since then, they have been a staple of the genre. In the early 70's there was a larger push to start making mechs even more humanoid than ever before, and following the releases of Mazinger Z, there was a huge shift to take mecha and their designs to a whole new level with series like Mobile Suit Gundam redefining what a mecha could even be. Thus, the mecha genre has entered a new era.
