What is ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a broad term for computer systems designed to perform tasks that often involve abilities associated with human thinking, such as recognizing patterns, understanding language, making predictions, or generating content.

Generative AI

Systems such as Large Language Models or LLMs that analyze vast amounts of training data to create new content such as text, code, images, or music. Unlike what many would count as traditional AI, which only analyzes or classifies existing data, generative AI tends to synthesize outputs based on prompts given to them.

What does RESPONSIBLE USE mean?

Responsible AI use can mean a lot of things. It can mean using it with care, good judgement, and especially accountability so that you can prevent harm. Using these technologies can warp people's ability to percieve what is fake or real, and praciticing responsible use can mean that one must be able to recognize when an AI's output is misleading; what an AI produces should not simply be accepted as the truth.

Helpful Note:

Artificial Intelligence is NOT a single standalone product. A chatbot, recommendation system, image generator, and automated hiring tool may work in different ways and raise different questions. You should always look at the purpose and policies of the specific tool before using them.
