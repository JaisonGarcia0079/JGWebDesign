What the lack of PRIVACY means and what it could lead to.

AI tools can produce helpful summaries and suggestions, but they can also lead to numerous mistakes, reflect bias, or generate convincing statements that are not supported by facts and evidence. Some tools may also collect or retain information users submit without them even knowing.

THINK Before Sharing

Don't enter passwords, private records, financial details, or any other sensitive information unless you understand the tool's data practices and have permission. Always think before doing so.

CHECK Important Claims

Make sure to verify specific dates, quotations, statistics, citations, and factual claims with reliable sources—especially when decisions depend soley on that important information.

SEARCH for Different Perspectives

Outputs can reflect gaps or biases in data and design. Consider who is represented and who may be left out.

Never treat an AI-generated citation, quotation, or source title as real until you have opened and checked it yourself!
