PRODUCTION INFORMATION

After the massive failure of Studio Gainax's debut film ROYAL SPACE FORCE: THE WINGS OF HONNÊAMISE, the studio was in loads of trouble and financial burden. To pay off their massive debts, co-founder of the studio Toshio Okada pitched a commercial project meant to appeal to the consumer tastes of the 80's otaku: pretty girls piloting giant robots. What could go wrong? At it's core, it very much is a parody of the classic tennis manga/anime series AIM FOR THE ACE! with a hint of aesthetics akin to the 1986 American Blockbuster TOP GUN.

Hideaki Anno at the time was a popular animator who worked on films like NAUSICAA OF THE VALLEY OF THE WIND, and THE SUPER DIMENSION FORTRESS MACROSS. Anno was asked to return to Gainax to work on this new series, but only agreed on the specifc terms that he could be the director. While Anno had some trouble while directing the first two episodes of the series, he became obsessed with how he wanted to handle the direction of the series past that point. The tone shifted and diverged away from paraody and leaned more into the hard sci-fi elements that many praised the series for.

Air Date: July 10, 1988 to July 7, 1989

Episode Duration: 6 episodes

Directors: Hideaki Anno, Ken Andou, Kazuya Tsurumaki

Studio: Gainax

STORY

In the very near future, a race of huge, insect-like aliens is discovered traveling the galaxy. These aliens seem dedicated to the eradication of the human species as it takes its first steps away from the solar system, and they are getting closer and closer to Earth. Humanity has responded by developing spacegoing battleships and giant fighting robots. These robots are piloted by the best and brightest of Earth's youth, picked from training schools around the world.

The story begins in the year 2023, not long after the first battles with the aliens, and centers on young Noriko Takaya. Although Noriko's father was a famous Captain in the space fleet who was killed during one of the first battles of the war, her own talents as a pilot are questionable. Nonetheless, she has entered a training school. Through the series Noriko, joined by the beautiful and talented Kazumi Amano, will fight to overcome the trauma of war, the doubts of her peers, and her own lack of confidence.
